• You can see which balloons can be popped by pressing on a balloon and holding while moving your finger around. This is a great way to find a SUPER-POP!
  • You can use the target to catch multiple prizes if you can position it between two prizes that are falling close together.
  • Practice moving the target while also tilting the prize toward it to maximize your bonus points!
  • If you have turned off instruction messages and would like to see them again, you can reset the messages in the Options menu.
  • If you run out of moves but have at least 1 powerup, you will have 5 seconds to use it before the game ends.
  • Try popping clusters of matching balloons on the top rows first and work your way down.
  • Save your powerups if you can. You will receive extra bonus points if you clear all the prizes and have powerups left.
  • Try to pop balloons with a star on them first. These contain powerups that can help you pop more balloons!
  • Remember, there are 3 skill levels to try: Easy, Medium, and Hard. The harder the difficulty, the higher the prizes will be.
  • The Undo button is a great way to take back a move. If you need to, you can continue to undo all the way back to the beginning of the game.
