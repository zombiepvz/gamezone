Skill Levels
 
 
 

Changing the Skill Level changes the difficulty of the game.

Difficulty
 Tile Drop Speed Honey Jar Size Bonus Game Value
Easy Slow 50 75
Medium Medium 100 100
Hard Fast 150 150

Note that short words are worth even less honey on Hard. For example, a 3-letter word on Easy is worth 2 honey, and that same 3-letter word on Hard is only worth 1 honey.
