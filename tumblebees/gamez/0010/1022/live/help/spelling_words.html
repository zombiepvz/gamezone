Spelling Words
 

The first letter of a word can be any tile. Each following letter must touch any previous letter in the word. Each tile that you select will highlight to show that it is part of the current word. Adjacent tiles will also slightly highlight to show they may be selected as part of this word.

  
 
 
 

If you want to remove letters from the word, click on a highlighted letter tile to back up. All letters from the end of the word to that letter (including the letter you clicked) will be removed from the word.

If you click on a letter tile that does not touch any of the tiles in your current word, you will start a new word with that letter. You can also click in any empty part of the honeycomb to deselect all of your highlighted letters.

When you have clicked all the letters in your word in the proper order, click the Submit button 		 
 
 
 

To submit a word, you may do any of the following:

  • Click the Submit button
  • Click the right mouse button
  • Press the enter key
  • Click the left mouse button while holding down the control key (or apple key for Macintosh.)

Spelling longer words is better, and the green tiles give you extra bonus points.
